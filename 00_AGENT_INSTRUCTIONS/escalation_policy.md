# Escalation Policy

## Escalate When

Escalation is required or strongly recommended when:

- The issue impacts production.
- The knowledge base has no matching case.
- Customer-specific approval is required.
- The request involves credentials or security configuration.
- The integration behavior is not documented.
- There is risk of data loss or duplicate data.
- The change affects candidate status, offer status, or downstream systems.
- The user is asking for an undocumented workaround.
- The solution may affect multiple customers.
- Required context is missing and the user wants to proceed anyway.

## Escalation Output

When escalation is needed, include:

- Why escalation is needed
- What information to collect
- Suggested owner or team
- Recommended next step
- Risk level
- Whether production is impacted
